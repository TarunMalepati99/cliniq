# Describing prior lines of therapy

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Eligibility criteria often specify the number and type of prior systemic regimens, for example treatment-naive in the metastatic setting, at least one prior platinum-containing regimen, or progression on prior immune checkpoint blockade. Medication lists in electronic records can be incomplete when treatment was given at another institution, so a patient with no recorded anticancer medication is better described as having unknown treatment history than as treatment-naive.
