# ECOG performance status scale

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

The ECOG (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group) performance status grades functional ability from 0 to 5: 0 fully active; 1 restricted in strenuous activity but ambulatory and able to do light work; 2 ambulatory and capable of self-care but unable to work, up and about more than half of waking hours; 3 capable of only limited self-care, confined to bed or chair more than half of waking hours; 4 completely disabled; 5 dead. Many interventional oncology trials require ECOG 0–1, and some allow 0–2. In routine records, performance status is frequently documented only in free-text notes.
