# Acquired resistance to EGFR TKIs and the T790M mutation

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Most tumors initially sensitive to first- or second-generation EGFR TKIs (for example erlotinib, gefitinib, afatinib) eventually progress. The EGFR T790M gatekeeper mutation has been reported as the most frequent single resistance mechanism after these agents, found in roughly half of resistant cases in several series. Third-generation inhibitors such as osimertinib were designed to inhibit T790M-positive disease. Because T790M is acquired, it is typically assessed on re-biopsy or plasma testing after progression, so its absence from an initial diagnostic panel says little about its status later in treatment.
