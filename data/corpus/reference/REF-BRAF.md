# BRAF V600E across tumor types

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

BRAF V600 mutations occur in roughly half of cutaneous melanomas and less frequently in colorectal cancer and NSCLC. Combined BRAF and MEK inhibition is used in BRAF V600E-mutant melanoma and NSCLC, while in colorectal cancer BRAF inhibition is typically combined with anti-EGFR antibodies because of feedback EGFR signaling.
