# HER2-positive breast cancer

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

HER2 (ERBB2) protein overexpression or gene amplification is present in a minority of breast cancers, often cited at about 15–20%. HER2 status is typically assessed by immunohistochemistry with reflex in situ hybridization for equivocal results. HER2-targeted antibodies such as trastuzumab carry a risk of cardiac dysfunction, so trials of HER2-directed therapy often exclude patients with significant heart failure or reduced ejection fraction.
