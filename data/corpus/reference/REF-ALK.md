# ALK rearrangements in NSCLC

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

ALK gene rearrangements, most often EML4-ALK fusions, are present in a small percentage of NSCLC and are enriched in younger patients and people with little or no smoking history. ALK inhibitors such as alectinib, brigatinib and lorlatinib are standard options for ALK-positive disease. Brain metastases are relatively common in ALK-positive NSCLC, so central nervous system criteria in ALK trials deserve close attention.
