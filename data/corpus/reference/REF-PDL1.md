# PD-L1 tumor proportion score (TPS)

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

The PD-L1 tumor proportion score is the percentage of viable tumor cells showing partial or complete membrane staining on immunohistochemistry. In NSCLC, scores are commonly grouped as below 1%, 1–49%, and 50% or greater, and a TPS of at least 50% is often described as high expression. Different antibody clones and scoring systems exist, so trial criteria usually specify the assay. A missing PD-L1 result should be treated as unknown rather than negative.
