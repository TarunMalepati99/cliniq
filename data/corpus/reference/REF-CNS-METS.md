# Brain metastases in trial eligibility

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Historically, many oncology trials excluded patients with brain metastases. Current practice increasingly permits patients with treated, clinically stable brain metastases while still excluding untreated or symptomatic lesions or leptomeningeal disease. When estimating feasibility from real-world data, brain imaging may be absent from structured records, so absence of a brain-metastasis diagnosis code is weak evidence that a patient has none.
