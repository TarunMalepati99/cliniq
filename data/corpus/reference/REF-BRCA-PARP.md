# BRCA1/2 pathogenic variants and PARP inhibition

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Pathogenic variants in BRCA1 or BRCA2 impair homologous recombination repair. PARP inhibitors such as olaparib exploit this defect through synthetic lethality and are used in BRCA-associated breast, ovarian, pancreatic and prostate cancers. Trials may require germline testing, tumor testing, or either, and often distinguish BRCA1 from BRCA2.
