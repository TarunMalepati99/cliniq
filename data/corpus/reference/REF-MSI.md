# Microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) and mismatch repair deficiency

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

MSI-high status, usually resulting from mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR), produces a high mutational burden and is associated with responsiveness to PD-1 blockade. It is found in a minority of colorectal cancers, more often in early-stage than metastatic disease, and in smaller fractions of many other tumor types. MSI-H/dMMR has supported tissue-agnostic approvals for checkpoint inhibitors.
