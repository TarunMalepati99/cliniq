# EGFR sensitizing mutations in NSCLC: exon 19 deletions and L858R

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Activating mutations in the EGFR kinase domain occur in a meaningful minority of lung adenocarcinomas, with higher frequency reported in East Asian patients and in people who never smoked. In-frame deletions in exon 19 and the L858R point substitution in exon 21 together account for the large majority of these sensitizing alterations. Tumors carrying either alteration are generally sensitive to EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs). Eligibility criteria for EGFR-directed studies therefore commonly name exon 19 deletion and L858R explicitly, and may require documentation from a validated tissue or plasma assay.

For feasibility work, it matters whether a patient was tested and found negative, or was never tested: an untested patient cannot be assumed to be EGFR wild-type.
