# Renal function criteria: creatinine and estimated GFR

AUTHORED REFERENCE NOTE — a short summary written for this demonstration from general domain knowledge. Figures are approximate and are not a substitute for primary literature or clinical guidance.

Serum creatinine is reported in mg/dL in the United States and in µmol/L in many other countries; 1 mg/dL corresponds to about 88.4 µmol/L. Trials express renal eligibility either as a creatinine threshold, a multiple of the upper limit of normal, or a creatinine clearance or estimated GFR (in mL/min or mL/min/1.73 m²). Thresholds defined relative to the upper limit of normal cannot be evaluated without each laboratory's reference range.
